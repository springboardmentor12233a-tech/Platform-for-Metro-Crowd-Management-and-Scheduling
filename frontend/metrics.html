💡 Metric Interpretation & Academic Presentation Reference

Standardized definitions used during mentor evaluation and project viva:

Accuracy (90.6%)

Overall proportion of correctly classified passenger density states across all stations and time-steps.

Precision (90.5%)

How many predicted high-congestion/critical alerts were true overcrowding events, minimizing false alarms.

Recall (90.6%)

Ability of the system to capture real crowding spikes, preventing unmitigated platform bottlenecking.

F1 Score (90.5%)

Balanced harmonic score ($2 \times \frac{P \times R}{P + R}$), validating reliability under class imbalance.

Forecast Accuracy (94.3%)

Near-term passenger footfall forecasting accuracy during morning and evening peak travel periods.
